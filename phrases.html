Greetings

おはようございます

ohayō gozaimasu

Good morning

Kansaiおはようさんohayō-san

Kansai note: Adding さん softens the greeting; you will hear it from neighbours and shopkeepers across Kansai.

Greetings

こんにちは

konnichiwa

Hello / welcome (Osaka style)

Kansaiまいどmaido

Kansai note: Literally “every time”, used by Osaka merchants as both hello and thanks. まいどおおきに means “thanks as always”.

Greetings

こんばんは

konbanwa

Good evening

Kansaiこんばんはsame

Kansai note: Evenings still start with こんばんは; in a shop you may hear まいど instead. The final は sounds like “wa”.

Greetings

はじめまして。よろしくおねがいします。

hajimemashite. yoroshiku onegaishimasu.

Nice to meet you.

Kansaiはじめまして。よろしゅう。hajimemashite. yoroshū.

Kansai note: The short Kansai closing is friendly and safe; use the full form for formal occasions.

Greetings

よろしくおねがいします。

yoroshiku onegaishimasu.

Pleased to meet you (polite)

Kansaiよろしゅうおたのもうしますyoroshū otanōshimasu

Kansai note: A traditional Kansai courtesy; よろしゅう alone is enough with friends. Also useful at the end of an email or request.

Greetings

ありがとうございます。

arigatō gozaimasu.

Thank you

Kansaiおおきにōkini

Kansai note: The signature Kansai thank-you. Shops say まいどおおきに; おおきに、ありがとうさん is warmer still.

Greetings

じゃあね / さようなら。

jā ne / sayōnara.

See you

Kansaiほな、またhona, mata

Kansai note: ほな is the everyday “well then”. ほなね is softer; さいなら sounds old-fashioned.

Essentials

すみません。

sumimasen.

Excuse me / sorry

Kansaiすんませんsunmasen

Kansai note: The Osaka contraction, and the most useful word on this page. Use it to call a server, stop a stranger, or apologise for a bumped elbow.

Essentials

おねがいします。

onegaishimasu.

Please

Kansaiたのんますtanonmasu

Kansai note: A polite Kansai request; put it after what you want: これ、たのんます。It works in shops, taxis and cafés.

Essentials

はい / いいえ。

hai / iie.

Yes / no

Kansaiええ / いやē / iya

Kansai note: ええ means yes or “good”; いや is a plain no, so soften it with だいじょうぶです when refusing something.

Essentials

わかりません。

wakarimasen.

I don't understand

Kansaiわかりまへんwakarimahen

Kansai note: The polite Kansai negative. With friends it shortens to わからへん; both are worth recognising.

Essentials

にほんごがはなせません。

nihongo ga hanasemasen.

I can't speak Japanese

Kansaiにほんごがはなせまへんnihongo ga hanasemahen

Kansai note: Honest and polite; locals usually slow down or switch to simpler Japanese. Pair it with すんません.

Essentials

えいご、はなせますか

eigo, hanasemasu ka

Can you speak English?

Kansaiえいご、はなせますかsame

Kansai note: Add すんません first. Casual Kansai: えいご、はなせる？ In cities the answer is often a little English.

Essentials

トイレはどこですか。

toire wa doko desu ka

Where is the toilet?

Kansaiトイレはどこでっかtoire wa doko dekka

Kansai note: でっか is the Osaka question ending: friendly, but casual. Add すんません to soften it.

Food & drink

これをください。

kore o kudasai.

This one, please

Kansaiこれ、おくれやすkore, okureyasu

Kansai note: A Kyoto-style polite request understood across Kansai; これ、たのんます is the Osaka-friendly alternative.

Food & drink

おすすめはなんですか。

osusume wa nan desu ka

What do you recommend?

Kansaiなんがおすすめですかnan ga osusume desu ka

Kansai note: Kansai なん for “what”; casual なんがおすすめ？ You may get an answer longer than you can follow — nod, then point.

Food & drink

おみずをください。

omizu o kudasai.

Water, please

Kansaiおみず、おくれやすomizu, okureyasu

Kansai note: Or おみず、たのんます。In many restaurants, water or tea arrives without asking.

Food & drink

おいしいです。

oishii desu.

It's delicious

Kansaiうまいですumai desu

Kansai note: うまい is the everyday Kansai praise; めっちゃうまい is the casual version. Say it to the cook and watch them beam.

Food & drink

いただきます / ごちそうさまでした。

itadakimasu / gochisōsamadeshita.

Thanks for the food / thanks for the meal

Kansaiいただきます / ごちそうさんitadakimasu / gochisō-san

Kansai note: Say the first before eating and the second after. ごちそうさん is the everyday Kansai form.

Food & drink

おかいけいおねがいします。

okaikei onegaishimasu.

The bill, please

Kansaiおかいけい、おくれやすokaikei, okureyasu

Kansai note: At many places you pay at the till by the door; ごちそうさん as you leave is always welcome.

Transport

すみません、えきはどこですか。

sumimasen, eki wa doko desu ka

Excuse me, where's the station?

Kansaiすんません、えきはどこでっかsunmasen, eki wa doko dekka

Kansai note: The all-purpose opener plus でっか. Swap えき for バスてい (bus stop) or コンビニ.

Transport

なんばまでおねがいします。

nanba made onegaishimasu.

To Namba, please (taxi)

Kansaiなんばまで、たのんますNamba made, tanonmasu

Kansai note: Put any destination before まで. たのんます is the friendly Kansai “please” and works in taxis, cafés and shops.

Transport

きっぷはどこで売っていますか。

kippu wa doko de utte imasu ka

Where do they sell tickets?

Kansaiきっぷはどこで売ってまっかkippu wa doko de uttemakka

Kansai note: まっか is the polite Osaka form of ますか. In big stations the answer is usually a machine behind you.

Transport

ここでとめてください。

koko de tomete kudasai.

Please stop here (taxi)

Kansaiここで、とめといておくれやすkoko de, tometoite okureyasu

Kansai note: 〜といて is the Kansai “do it in advance / just do it” helper. Point at the kerb as you say it.

Transport

のりかえはどこですか。

norikae wa doko desu ka

Where do I change trains?

Kansaiのりかえはどこでっかnorikae wa doko dekka

Kansai note: Add the line name for clarity: 御堂筋線へのりかえはどこでっか.

Shopping

いくらですか。

ikura desu ka

How much is it?

Kansaiなんぼですかnanbo desu ka

Kansai note: なんぼ is the Kansai “how much” and one of the words people listen for. Casual: なんぼ？

Shopping

見せてもらってもいいですか。

misete moratte mo ii desu ka

Could I have a look?

Kansai見せてもらってもええでっかmisete moratte mo ē dekka

Kansai note: ええ = good / okay, so 〜てもええ = “is it okay to…”. Very useful with でっか at the end.

Shopping

カードはつかえますか

kādo wa tsukaemasu ka

Can I use a card?

Kansaiカードはつかえますかsame

Kansai note: Casual Kansai: カード、使える？ Small shops and markets are often cash only, so it is worth asking first.

Shopping

ふくろはいりません。

fukuro wa irimasen.

No bag needed

Kansaiふくろ、いらんわfukuro, iran wa

Kansai note: いらん is the Kansai negative of いる (“need”). The trailing わ is soft, not rude — but use the standard form in a formal shop.

Emergency

たすけてください

tasukete kudasai

Help me, please

Kansaiたすけてくださいsame

Kansai note: Emergencies are no time for dialect. Shout たすけて！ if it is urgent. Police 110, ambulance and fire 119.

Emergency

きゅうきゅうしゃをよんでください。

kyūkyūsha o yonde kudasai

Please call an ambulance

Kansaiきゅうきゅうしゃ、よんどくれやすkyūkyūsha, yondo kureyasu

Kansai note: くれやす is the polite Kansai “please do”. If you cannot speak, show this line on your phone.

Emergency

びょういんはどこですか。

byōin wa doko desu ka

Where's the hospital?

Kansaiびょういんはどこでっかbyōin wa doko dekka

Kansai note: For a clinic, say クリニック; for a pharmacy, くすりや. Add ちかくの (nearby) at the front.

Emergency

パスポート、なくしました

pasupōto, nakushimashita

I've lost my passport

Kansaiパスポート、なくしましたsame

Kansai note: Casual Kansai: パスポート、なくしてん。Report it at a こうばん (police box) first, then your embassy.

Small talk

おなまえはなんですか

onamae wa nan desu ka

What's your name?

Kansaiおなまえはなんですかsame

Kansai note: Casual Kansai: おなまえ、なんて言うの？ Answer with 〜です, or just your name and よろしゅう.

Small talk

どこから来ましたか。

doko kara kimashita ka

Where are you from?

Kansaiどこから来たんdoko kara kitan

Kansai note: The casual Kansai 〜たん ending is common between new acquaintances. Polite version: どこから来はりましたん.

Small talk

にほんごがじょうずですね。

nihongo ga jōzu desu ne.

Your Japanese is good!

Kansaiにほんご、じょうずやなあnihongo, jōzu yanā

Kansai note: 〜やなあ is the Kansai “isn’t it” — warm and casual. You will hear this a lot; the modest reply is まだまだです.
